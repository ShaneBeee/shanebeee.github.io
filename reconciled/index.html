Reconciled

A personal desktop app for self-employed professionals and sole proprietors — track hours, log kilometrage, manage expenses, generate invoices, and prepare CRA-ready tax documentation, all in one place.

Usage Guide Dev Notes View on GitHub ↗

What it does

Reconciled started life as a simple hours-and-invoicing tool and grew into a full bookkeeping suite. It's built specifically around the reality of being an independent contractor in Canada — CRA T2125 categories, GST tracking, CCA asset depreciation, tax bracket set-asides — without needing an accounting degree to use it.

🏠

Dashboard

At-a-glance stats for the month, year-to-date, and year-over-year comparisons.

📅

Work Logs

Calendar-based time tracking with multi-boss splitting and KM/extras entries.

🧾

Invoicing

Generate, email, and track invoices with automatic tax set-aside calculations.

💸

Expenses & CCA

T2125-aligned expense categories plus capital asset depreciation tracking.

🚗

Kilometre Log

Business-use tracking for vehicle expense deductions, auto-synced from work logs.

📊

Accounting Exports

Excel, CSV, and full PDF tax reports ready to hand to your accountant.

Who it's for

Built primarily for self-employed contractors and sole proprietors filing under CRA's T2125 form — realtors, freelancers, tradespeople, or anyone juggling multiple clients and their own books. It also supports multiple independent user profiles on the same install, so more than one person can use it without their data ever mixing.
